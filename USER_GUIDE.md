# PTC Enrollment System - User Guide

## Quick Start

### For Students

1. **Opening the System**
   - Open `index.html` in your web browser
   - Click "Student Portal" button

2. **Account Creation**
   - Click "Sign Up" tab
   - Enter your full name, email, and password (minimum 6 characters)
   - Confirm your password
   - Click "Create Account"

3. **First Login**
   - Enter your email and password
   - You'll be redirected to enrollment type selection

4. **Select Your Enrollment Type**
   - Choose one of:
     - **Freshmen**: First-time enrollees
     - **Regular**: Continuing students
     - **Transferee**: Students from other institutions
   - Click "Confirm & Proceed"

5. **Update Your Profile**
   - Click "My Profile" in the sidebar
   - Fill in all your personal information:
     - Full Name, Email, Phone
     - Date of Birth, Gender, Civil Status
     - Address details
   - Click "Save Profile"

6. **Submit Your Requirements**
   - Click "Submit Requirements" in the sidebar
   - Select your course
   - Upload your documents:
     - Form 138 / Diploma (Required)
     - Birth Certificate (PSA)
     - Certificate of Good Moral
     - Valid Government ID
   - Click "Review & Submit"
   - Review your files
   - Click "Confirm & Submit"

7. **Check Your Status**
   - Click "Notifications" to see status updates
   - Admin will review your documents
   - Status will change to "Approved" or "Rejected"
   - Once approved, "My Subjects" will show your enrolled subjects

### For Admins

1. **Login**
   - Open `index.html`
   - Click "Admin Portal"
   - Enter ID: `admin`
   - Enter Password: `admin123`
   - Click "Login"

2. **Dashboard Overview**
   - View key statistics:
     - Total Applicants
     - Pending Applications
     - Approved Students
     - Rejected Applications
   - See recent notifications and enrollments

3. **Manage Students**
   - Click "Manage Students"
   - View all student records
   - Use search to find students by name, email, or ID
   - Filter by status or enrollment type
   - Click "Edit" to change student status
   - Click "Delete" to remove student records

4. **Verify Documents**
   - Click "Document Verification"
   - Review uploaded documents from students
   - Click "Approve" to accept documents
   - Click "Reject" to request resubmission
   - Click "View" to see document details
   - Students receive automatic notifications

5. **Process Applications**
   - Click "Applications"
   - View all enrollment applications
   - Filter by status or enrollment type
   - Click "Approve" to approve enrollment
   - Click "Reject" to reject enrollment
   - Students automatically notified

6. **Manage Courses**
   - Click "Courses & Subjects"
   - **Add Course**: Enter code, name, and description
   - **Add Subject**: Enter code, name, and units
   - View all courses and subjects
   - Delete courses/subjects as needed

7. **Monitor Notifications**
   - Click "Notifications"
   - View all system notifications
   - See student submissions and status changes
   - Click "Mark All Read" when done

## Document Status Meanings

### For Students
- **Pending**: Your document is under review
- **Approved**: Document accepted! ✅
- **Rejected**: Document needs to be resubmitted ❌

### Enrollment Status
- **Pending**: Application under review
- **Approved**: You're enrolled! ✅
- **Rejected**: Application not approved ❌

## Enrollment Types Explained

### Freshmen
- First-time enrollees at PTC
- Receive: CS101, MATH101, ENG101 courses

### Regular
- Continuing students from previous terms
- Receive: CS201, CS202 courses

### Transferee
- Students transferring from other institutions
- Receive: CS150, MATH150 bridging courses

## Tips & Tricks

### For Students
1. **Upload all documents at once** - Don't split submissions
2. **Check notifications regularly** - Status updates come here
3. **Review before submitting** - The review page lets you verify files
4. **Keep your profile updated** - Admins may need to contact you
5. **Wait for approval** - Subjects appear after enrollment approval

### For Admins
1. **Use filters** - Speed up finding specific students
2. **Check recent notifications** - See what needs attention
3. **Approve/Reject decisively** - Clear communications help
4. **Monitor pending applications** - Follow up on pending items
5. **Maintain course list** - Keep courses updated for accuracy

## Common Issues & Solutions

### "Invalid email or password"
- **Solution**: Check your email spelling and password. If you haven't signed up yet, click "Sign Up" first.

### "Please fill in all fields"
- **Solution**: Check the form and make sure all required fields (marked with *) are filled.

### "No subjects available"
- **Solution**: Your enrollment hasn't been approved yet by admin. Check notifications for approval status.

### Documents not appearing
- **Solution**: Wait for the admin to review. Check "Document Verification" section if you're admin.

### "An account with this email already exists"
- **Solution**: Use a different email to sign up, or login if you already have an account.

## Data Persistence

- All data is saved in your browser's local storage
- Data persists across sessions
- Clearing browser cache will erase all data
- Private/Incognito mode doesn't persist data

## Logging Out

- Click the logout button in the sidebar
- You'll be returned to the home page
- Your data remains saved for next login

## Browser Requirements

- Modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- JavaScript enabled
- LocalStorage enabled
- Minimum screen width: 320px (mobile)

## File Size Limits

- Individual documents: up to 5MB
- Recommended formats: PDF, JPG, PNG
- Files are stored in browser memory only
